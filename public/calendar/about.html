RedSecCal

RedSecCal is the operations planner for RedSecTools. It uses a dashboard layout with focused sidebar views for personal schedules, project management, team delivery planning, and utilisation statistics.

Users can manage their own all-day, multi-day, and timed calendar items, while elevated calendar roles can view team schedules, assign project time in daily or custom blocks, and manage project delivery windows from the same tool.

Projects support estimates in hours or days, daily billable rates, automatic estimated-cost rollups, scheduled-versus-completed progress tracking, and utilisation reporting across weekly, monthly, and yearly windows.
