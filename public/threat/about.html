RedSecThreat

RedSecThreat is the threat intelligence monitor for RedSecTools. It continuously collects and analyses data from configurable feed sources, watching for keywords and patterns that matter to your security operations.

Feeds are ingested from RSS, APIs, websites, and onion services. Each feed is monitored on a configurable schedule and scanned against keyword rules supporting both plain text and regular expression matching. Matched results are enriched with automatic IOC extraction and assigned criticality levels for triage.

Alerts route through configurable notification channels including webhooks, email, and Discord integrations. Tags provide a flexible organising layer across feeds, keywords, and alerts for filtering and reporting.

Feed Sources

Configure feed sources from RSS feeds, websites, REST APIs, and Tor onion services. Each source runs on its own polling schedule and normalises content into a unified stream for keyword scanning.

Keyword Monitoring

Define keyword rules using plain text matching or full regular expression patterns. Rules are evaluated against every ingested item and grouped into logical sets for targeted alerting.

Alert System

Matched items generate alerts with assigned criticality levels and automatic IOC extraction for IPs, domains, hashes, URLs, and email addresses. Alerts are deduplicated and correlated across feeds to reduce noise.

Notifications

Route alerts to the right people through webhook, email, and Discord notification channels. Each channel can be filtered by criticality level, feed source, or keyword match set.

Tags

Apply tags to feeds, keywords, and alerts for cross-cutting organisation. Filter dashboards and reports by tag to focus on specific threat categories, campaigns, or operational contexts.
